Curatorial Line

Mixi is looking for creators who explore the ultra-contemporary through powerful expressions that embrace pop culture, surrealism, the uncanny, liminality, the absurd, hybrid identities, and speculative narratives as powerful languages of our current era.

We are dedicated to the experimental exploration of the present, embracing transdisciplinary practices.

Who Can Apply?

Individual Artists

submitting individual works or a full exhibition project

Artist Collectives

submitting an exhibition project

Curators

submitting an exhibition project

Accepted Media

Painting, Sculpture, Ceramics, Textiles, Video Art, Installation, and other innovative or transdisciplinary proposals.

This open call is open to all artists worldwide.

Benefits

For Selected Participants

01Participation in curated exhibitions within Mixi's Fall 2026 – Spring 2027 Exhibition Cycle
02Strategic visibility across Mixi's digital platforms (Instagram, newsletter, and website)
03Professional digital catalog featuring the selected works for each exhibition
04Sales opportunities through Mixi
05Possibility to participate in international events in Mexico City and national / international art fairs
06Personalized advisory and mentorship from the Mixi team
