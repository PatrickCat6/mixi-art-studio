Our Services

Personalized guidance for artistic career development, market positioning, and medium to long-term strategic planning.

Design and production of professional portfolios, both physical and digital, optimized for galleries, residencies, open calls, and sales.

Comprehensive support preparing and submitting applications, document review, proposal writing, artist statements, and selection of visual material for each opportunity.

High-resolution photography sessions to document artworks, ideal for personal archives, catalogs, applications, and digital promotion.

Writing and refinement of artistic statements, professional biographies, and supporting texts for exhibitions and applications.

Advice on building the artist's brand identity and strategies to increase visibility on social media, websites, and specialized art platforms.

Support for participation in national and international art fairs, artwork selection, booth design, negotiation, and commercial follow-up.

Analysis and establishment of appropriate pricing based on the artist's career, market, and type of work, along with commercialization strategies.

Design of content and digital promotion strategies to increase visibility and generate sales and collaboration opportunities.

Guidance on copyright, exhibition contracts, consignment agreements, and other legal aspects related to the artistic career.

Comprehensive design of exhibition presentation and installation, spatial layout, lighting, museography, and artwork placement to create an optimal viewer experience.

Development of complete curatorial proposals for individual and group exhibitions.

Availability of our professional exhibition space with optimal lighting and promotional conditions. Explore it in 3D and in the interactive floorplan below.

Specialized consulting for collectors on the organization, cataloging, conservation, and management of their art collections.
